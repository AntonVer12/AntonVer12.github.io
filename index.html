Hi, I'm Antoni.

I'm a game designer interested in immersive experiences, atmospheric worlds and storytelling through gameplay. I enjoy developing ideas from an initial concept into playable experiences, combining narrative, level design and gameplay systems. Additionally, I am currently studying game design and writing my master's thesis on designing mechanics based on data reinterpretation as a foundation for multi-layered, non-chronological game narratives.

I've worked on team-based projects and game jams, contributing to creative direction, design documentation, prototyping, dialogue writing and implementation in Unity. I'm especially interested in survival horror and narrative-driven games.
