01 · The Project

Combat Design as Storytelling

DreamDeck is a narrative-driven card game about loss and confronting its consequences. The game weaves together grounded, realistic scenarios — such as interrogations and newspaper clippings — with levels that gradually descend into an increasingly oneiric dream world, a place Lucy retreats to.

But what is this dream world, really? The narrative plays with the player's perception and understanding. Exploration segments are complemented by a combat system based on collecting cards that are deeply tied to Lucy's emotional state.

These mechanics form an additional layer of storytelling, where delving into the protagonist's psyche directly rewards the player. Those who pay close attention to the narrative can leverage their understanding to create powerful combat combinations.

03 · Reading the Clues

Discovering Card Synergies Through Exploration

The player's true strength in combat lies in discovering subtly hidden clues during the exploration segments that hint at card combinations, and using them effectively in battle.

The following example shows how an exploration clue can help the player identify a useful card combination.

Example 1 · The Hint

Discovering the Clue

English Translation

Lucy, on the last pages of "Revega" I hid my sketches. I hid a broken dream guardian there and added new dragons! Let me know when you see them! – Simon

Design Significance

The note found in the world hints at a card combination that can be discovered by the player.

Example 2 · Card A

Fantasy Novel "Revega"

English Translation

HYBRID · Focus x2 · Narrative

An old novel full of colorful descriptions of Revega, which Lucy's mother used to read to her in the evenings.

Role in the Combination

Found on her father’s bookshelf, Lucy suspects this book is the source of her nightmares. Played alone, it heals and draws cards.

Example 3 · Card B

Simon's Crayons

English Translation

ATTACK · Magic · Creativity

They were everywhere - on the table, under the bed, in his jacket pockets. Their purpose is endless.

Role in the Combination

A card discovered in Lucy’s dreamworld after a pivotal narrative moment. When used alone, its sole effect is dealing damage.

Design Takeaway

The cards work pretty well on their own, but the player is rewarded for paying attention to the narrative and discovering the hidden combination, which helps him use the story as his advantage in combat. This encourages exploration and engagement with the story, making the combat system an integral part of the overall experience.
