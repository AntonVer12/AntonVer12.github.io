A Cosy Mushroom-Gardening Strategy Game

Protecting the magical forest from ruthless Foragers by expanding mycelium networks and managing garden resources.

01 · Concept & Responsibilities

My Role & Scope of Work

Developed during a 7-day game jam, *FunGone* required fast scoping, creative worldbuilding, and precise mechanical balance.

📖 Narrative Design & Writing

  • Created foundational world lore and forest symbiosis background.
  • Wrote short-form, humorous dialogue and character banters.
  • Framed the narrative around the existential threat of foragers.

🌿 Game Design & Systems

  • Designed grid-based roguelite gardening mechanics.
  • Structured resource management (Resin and Rerolls).
  • Balanced item pools and mycelium expansion loops.
02 · Character Writing & Dialogue Highlights

Characters & Written Lines

Writing for *FunGone* focused on snappy, lighthearted banter between woodland fungi facing foragers. Here are 3 key characters alongside lines written for them:

Character 01

Funia — The Undershroom

The determined protagonist trying to keep the mycelium network alive against incoming foragers.

"You step into the shroomy boots of a certain undershroom known by the name Funia, attempting to save the forest from the dreadful Foragers!"

Character 02

Sporesby — Woodland Neighbor

A nervous fungal resident anxious about space management and soil plot expansions.

"Careful where you drop your spores, stranger! Some of us are trying to establish a quiet symbiotic network before the basket comes down."

Character 03

Elder Mycelia

The wise old cap offering cryptic advice and tactical gardening tips to Funia.

"Symbiosis isn't just about sharing soil; it's about making sure the foragers pick moss instead of our prized mycelium caps!"

03 · Retrospective

What I Have Learned

01 · Concise & Punchy Writing in Jams

Working on a 7-day jam taught me how to deliver character personality through snappy, short-form dialogue lines without bogging down the player with excessive text boxes.

02 · Balancing Cosy Themes with Strategic Depth

Designing a lighthearted aesthetic alongside grid-based roguelite mechanics required careful balance to ensure the gameplay remained engaging and tactical without losing its charming vibe.

03 · Rapid Scoping Under Tight Deadlines

Narrowing down core mechanics early in the week allowed me to integrate narrative elements and resource systems smoothly before the jam deadline hit.
