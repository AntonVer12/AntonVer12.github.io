Narrative Design Breakdown · Project N-O-L

01 · Worldbuilding & Environmental Storytelling

The Conspiracy Theorist's Basement (Blockout & Layout)

Created during a week-long game jam, Project N-O-L places the player in the shoes of a UFO fanatic operating in communist-era Poland. Operating from a cramped, dreary basement in a concrete prefab block of flats (*wielka płyta*), the physical layout of the room is essential for storytelling.

I designed the blockout and layout of the basement to instantly communicate the authentic "conspiracy uncle" (*szuryczny wujaszek*) vibe through environmental design:

Zone 01

The Corkboard

Covered in interconnected notes, press clippings, and string mapping out alleged extraterrestrial sightings and government cover-ups.

Zone 02

The Side Desk

Scratched surfaces featuring nostalgic Polish pop-culture references of the era alongside emblems of the local football team the uncle passionately roots for.

Zone 03

The Main Operational Desk

The true heart of operations holding classified files, newspaper clippings, and the vital equipment needed to trigger contact.

Design Significance: Every prop and its placement tell a story of isolation, obsession, and historical context under communist rule.
02 · Narrative Flow & Puzzles

Weaving Clues Into Text Across 5 Endings

Designing the investigation required building a coherent puzzle flow where hints embedded directly within in-game texts (like newspapers and secret documents) guide the player toward specific thematic conclusions.

The game features **5 distinct endings**, each tied directly to how the player interprets clues and combines frequencies, signals, and items. Every hint maintains strict narrative consistency with the communist-era setting and the protagonist's eccentric worldview.

Example · Environmental Text Clue

"The mysterious night disturbance near the tractor factory was dismissed as industrial equipment malfunction, but workers reported rhythmic humming matching local broadcast frequencies..."

03 · Mechanics & Interaction

The Operational Base: Microwave, Bulbulator, and Radio

Once clues are gathered from newspapers, television signals, and radio broadcasts, the player interacts with eccentric hardware to attempt communication.

The core loop revolves around taking data extracted from environmental storytelling and translating it into settings for bizarre summoning devices.

Gameplay recording demonstrating how clues from documents are applied to equipment like the radio and microwave.

04 · Design Takeaways

What I Learned from Project N-O-L

Key Takeaway 01 · Audiovisual Cohesion

Audio must serve the narrative deeply. The music and broadcasts playing on the radio need authentic thematic context within the game's universe to sell the atmosphere effectively.

Key Takeaway 02 · Interior Layout & Worldbuilding

The spatial arrangement of a room is a powerful storytelling tool. Proper layout instantly communicates character psychology, historical context, and narrative tone without relying on exposition.

Key Takeaway 03 · Scoping & Player Guidance

In games featuring abstract settings and heavy reading elements, adding a clear, well-structured onboarding tutorial is critical to prevent player frustration and ensure smooth puzzle flow.
