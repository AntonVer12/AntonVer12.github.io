05 · Retrospective

What I Have Learned

01 · Designing for Unpredictable Player Behavior

Building an immersive sim taught me to abandon linear script assumptions. Levels must be resilient to creative player choices, requiring clear readability and robust systemic fallback paths.

02 · Production Leadership & Scoping

Managing a long-term student team reinforced the value of realistic milestone scoping, clear communication, and empowering team members while keeping the central creative vision intact.

03 · Technical Level Design & Lighting

Lighting isn't just visual polish—it is a critical navigation tool. Mastering Unity light setups, volumetric fog, and prefab workflows allowed me to guide player movement naturally without relying on artificial markers.

04 · Public Playtesting & Feedback Loop

Exhibiting at *Hello GameDev* highlighted the importance of watching raw, unguided gameplay. Observing players tackle encounters revealed critical insights into spatial navigation and UI feedback that informed our next sprint iterations.
